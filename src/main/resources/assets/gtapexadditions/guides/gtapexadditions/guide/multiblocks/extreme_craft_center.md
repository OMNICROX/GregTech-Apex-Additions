# Extreme Craft Center

<Multiblock id="gtapexadditions:extreme_craft_center" />

**Tier:** IV · **Kind:** Automation · **From:** Twist Space Technology

An ME crafting provider: crafting patterns run inside it with pattern magnification, so it acts like a huge set of molecular assemblers on your AE2 network.

## Building it

In the 3D view, left drag rotates, the scroll wheel zooms and right or middle drag pans; right-click a block to see what
that spot accepts. The buttons beside it zoom, reset the view, switch between shapes, show where hatches can go and
list every block you need; the sliders under it pick a tier or show one layer at a time. The
[Structure Upgrader](../items/structure-upgrader.md) builds it for you.
