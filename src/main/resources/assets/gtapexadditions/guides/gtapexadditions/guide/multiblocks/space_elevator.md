# Space Elevator

<Row>
  <BlockImage id="gtapexadditions:space_elevator" scale="3" />
</Row>

**Tier:** UHV · **Kind:** Space · **From:** GTNH

Elevates to space. Up to 12 modules: mining drones, pumps and the Space Assembler (which makes UIV+ components and GTNA's capstones).

## Building it

Press **U** on the controller in JEI for the 3D structure (layers, tiers, hatch spots, block counts), or use the
[Structure Upgrader](../items/structure-upgrader.md) to place it.
