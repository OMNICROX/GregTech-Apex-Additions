# Supercritical Steam Turbine

<Multiblock id="gtna:supercritical_steam_turbine" />

**From:** GTNA

**Runs:** Supercritical Steam Turbine

## What it does

* Main Function: An IV turbine that expands supercritical steam through a rotor into EU.
* Base production: 16384 EU/t. An installed rotor is required; output scales with the Rotor Holder.
* Each Rotor Holder tier above IV adds 10% efficiency and doubles the output. The supercritical module adds 2x output, +20% efficiency and a 2x rotor damage multiplier.
* Consumes Dense Supercritical Steam in place of GTO's Supercritical Steam; the fuel recipe keeps GTO's 80 mB → 8 mB distilled water numbers.
