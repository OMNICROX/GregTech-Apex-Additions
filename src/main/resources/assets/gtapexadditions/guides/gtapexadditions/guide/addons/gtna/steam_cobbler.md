**From:** GTNA

## What it does

* Steam Consumption: 1200 L/s (60 L/t)
* Main Function: Advanced Steam Rock Generator.
* Generates various stones based on Programmed Circuits.
* Max Parallel: 16 operations.
* Structure: 3x3x3 Cube with Bronze Pipe center.
