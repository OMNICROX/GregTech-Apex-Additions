# Industrial Rock Breaker

<Row>
  <BlockImage id="gtapexadditions:industrial_rock_breaker" scale="3" />
</Row>

**Tier:** MV · **Kind:** Processing · **From:** GT++ (GTNH)

Makes stone blocks from lava and water. A programmed circuit picks the block: 1 cobblestone, 2 stone, 3 obsidian (uses redstone), 4 basalt.

## Building it

Press **U** on the controller in JEI for the 3D structure (layers, tiers, hatch spots, block counts), or use the
[Structure Upgrader](../items/structure-upgrader.md) to place it.
