# Circuit Assembly Line

<Row>
  <BlockImage id="gtapexadditions:circuit_assembly_line" scale="3" />
</Row>

**Tier:** LuV · **Kind:** Assembly · **From:** GT++ / GTNH

Circuits in bulk. Imprinted by the first circuit it makes (imprints are permanent), it then makes that circuit from its parts much cheaper.

## Building it

Press **U** on the controller in JEI for the 3D structure (layers, tiers, hatch spots, block counts), or use the
[Structure Upgrader](../items/structure-upgrader.md) to place it.
