# Pseudostable Black Hole Containment Field

<Row>
  <BlockImage id="gtapexadditions:black_hole_compressor" scale="3" />
</Row>

**Tier:** UHV · **Kind:** Processing · **From:** GTNH

A compressor and neutronium compressor powered by a black hole. Insert a Black Hole Seed to open it at 100 stability; stability drops over time; a Black Hole Collapser closes it.

## Building it

Press **U** on the controller in JEI for the 3D structure (layers, tiers, hatch spots, block counts), or use the
[Structure Upgrader](../items/structure-upgrader.md) to place it.
