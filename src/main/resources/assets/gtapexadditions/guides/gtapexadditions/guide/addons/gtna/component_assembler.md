# Component Assembler

<Multiblock id="gtna:component_assembler" />

**From:** GTNA

**Runs:** Component Assembly

## What it does

* Main Function: Assembles batches of 16 electric components from materials and circuits.
* The tier of every component casing must match.
* The base structure supports recipes up to IV; a formed extension raises the cap to UV.
* Each batch needs the casing tier shown in its recipe and a numbered circuit.

> GTO's gift to new employees makes assembly feel like building blocks. The base handles recipes through IV; its extension reaches UV. Treat it well: your first salary may cost less than one of its parts.
