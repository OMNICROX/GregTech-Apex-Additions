# Steam Lava Maker

<Multiblock id="gtna:steam_lava_maker" />

**From:** GTNA

**Runs:** Lava Maker

## What it does

* Main Function: steam lava maker: melts stone into lava.
* Efficiency: runs on steam instead of EU.
* Parallel: Processes up to 16 recipes.
* Speed: 1 second per stone block.
