# ISA Mill

<Multiblock id="gtna:isa_mill" />

**From:** GTNA

**Runs:** Isa Mill

## What it does

* Main Function: Wet-grinds ores and raw ores into Milled products with a Perfect Overclock (duration ÷4 per voltage tier).
* A Ball Hatch with a matching grinding ball is required; every operation consumes ball durability.
* Soapstone balls (tier 1) process ore blocks; Aluminium balls (tier 2) halve the duration and yield fewer Milled products.
* Circuit 1 selects the tier-1 mode and circuit 10 selects the tier-2 mode.

> The Isa 1672N mill is green on the outside, but hardly eco-friendly. Its exhaust smells strange, and few workers want to touch the messy output. Wet ball milling crushes ores with remarkable efficiency. The scientists reluctantly admit that brute force sometimes works.
