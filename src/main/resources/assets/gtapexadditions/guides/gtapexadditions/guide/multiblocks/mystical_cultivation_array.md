# Mystical Cultivation Array

<Row>
  <BlockImage id="gtapexadditions:mystical_cultivation_array" scale="3" />
</Row>

**Tier:** LV · **Kind:** Farming · **From:** Apex

A crop farm for Mystical Agriculture and the GregTech crops. Seeds go in its Seed Hatches (up to 2) and are never used up; each seed is one crop. Its output bus spot also takes an ME Output Bus.

## Building it

Press **U** on the controller in JEI for the 3D structure (layers, tiers, hatch spots, block counts), or use the
[Structure Upgrader](../items/structure-upgrader.md) to place it.
