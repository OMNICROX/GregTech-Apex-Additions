# Steam Multiblocks

Steam multiblocks run steam-age recipes with **parallels**, long before you have electricity to spare. GTCEu has
two; GTNA adds the GT New Horizons steam machines.

| Machine | Recipes | Parallels | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| <ItemLink id="gtceu:steam_grinder" /> | Macerator | up to 8 (pack setting) | GTCEu; Steam Bus and Steam Hatch only |
| <ItemLink id="gtceu:steam_oven" /> | Furnace | up to 8 (pack setting) | GTCEu; Steam Bus and Steam Hatch only |
| <ItemLink id="gtna:large_steam_crusher" /> | Macerator | 128 | 2x speed, 80% steam |
| <ItemLink id="gtna:large_steam_furnace" /> | Furnace | 128 | 10x speed, 50% steam |
| <ItemLink id="gtna:large_steam_alloy_smelter" /> | Alloy Smelter | 64 | 43% faster |
| <ItemLink id="gtna:large_steam_compressor" /> | Compressor | 48 | 2.5x speed |
| <ItemLink id="gtna:large_steam_extractor" /> | Extractor | 48 | 75% faster |
| <ItemLink id="gtna:large_steam_hammer" /> | Forge Hammer | 64 | 2x speed |
| <ItemLink id="gtna:large_steam_mixer" /> | Mixer | 64 | 2x speed |
| <ItemLink id="gtna:large_steam_centrifuge" /> | Centrifuge | 64 | 3.5x speed |
| <ItemLink id="gtna:large_steam_thermal_centrifuge" /> | Thermal Centrifuge | 64 | 3x speed |
| <ItemLink id="gtna:large_steam_ore_washer" /> | Ore Washer | 96 | 5x speed |
| <ItemLink id="gtna:large_steam_bath" /> | Chemical Bath | 64 | 2x speed |
| <ItemLink id="gtna:large_steam_forming_press" /> | Forming Press | 32 | 2.5x speed, 40% steam |
| <ItemLink id="gtna:large_steam_bending" /> | Bender | 16 | 2x speed, 50% steam |
| <ItemLink id="gtna:large_steam_cutting" /> | Cutter | 16 | 2x speed, 50% steam |
| <ItemLink id="gtna:large_steam_extruder" /> | Extruder | 16 | 2x speed, 50% steam |
| <ItemLink id="gtna:large_steam_lathe" /> | Lathe | 16 | 2x speed, 50% steam |
| <ItemLink id="gtna:large_steam_wiremill" /> | Wiremill | 16 | 2x speed, 50% steam |
| <ItemLink id="gtna:large_steam_sifter" /> | Sifter | 16 | 2x speed, 50% steam |
| <ItemLink id="gtna:large_steam_circuit_assembler" /> | Circuit Assembler | 64 | engrave a circuit to target it |

* Steam goes in through a **Steam Hatch**; the GTCEu ones only accept **Steam Input/Output Buses**.
* Speeds are compared with the single-block steam machine; check each controller's tooltip for its structure.
* Feed them from a big boiler: a Large Bronze Boiler, or the GTNA **Large Steam Solar Boiler** and **Large Steam
  Storage Tank**.
