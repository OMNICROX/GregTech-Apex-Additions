# Blaze Blast Furnace

<Multiblock id="gtna:blaze_blast_furnace" />

**From:** GTNA

**Runs:** Electric Blast Furnace

## What it does

* Main Function: Runs Electric Blast Furnace recipes in up to 64 parallel at half duration.
* Consumes molten Blaze when a recipe starts and every second while working.
* Heating coil temperature must meet the recipe requirement.
* Blaze Casings use a Large Chemical Reactor recipe with the original ingredients.

> A new Tinkers' Construct employee kept a blaze spawner among the furnaces. The furnaces gained a coat of blaze residue and suddenly ran twice as fast. They could also process 64 items in a batch. The spawner was removed, but blaze acceleration stayed.
