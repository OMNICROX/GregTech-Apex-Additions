# Primitive Stone Furnace

<Multiblock id="gtna:primitive_stone_furnace" />

**From:** GTNA

**Runs:** Electric Furnace

## What it does

* Main Function: A primitive furnace carved from stone: smelts like a furnace, with no energy required.
* Feed items in and take the smelted results out; no power hatch is needed.
