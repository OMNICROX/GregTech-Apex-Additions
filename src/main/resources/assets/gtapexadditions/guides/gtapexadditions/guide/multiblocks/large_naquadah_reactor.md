# Large Naquadah Reactor

<Row>
  <BlockImage id="gtapexadditions:large_naquadah_reactor" scale="3" />
</Row>

**Tier:** IV · **Kind:** Power · **From:** GoodGenerator (GTNH)

Generates power from liquid nuclear fuel or liquid naquadah fuel. It needs liquid air every second or it voids the fuel; boosting fluids raise its output.

## Building it

Press **U** on the controller in JEI for the 3D structure (layers, tiers, hatch spots, block counts), or use the
[Structure Upgrader](../items/structure-upgrader.md) to place it.
